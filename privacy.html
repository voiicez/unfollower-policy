Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Operator and scope

Unfollowers (also listed as Unfollowers: No Login Tracker or Unfollowers No Login Required) and unfollowersnologin.com are operated by Behlül Şahin. This policy covers the iOS and Android app and this website. For privacy questions and requests, email tothecornerlabs@gmail.com.

No Unfollowers account or Instagram login is required. Instagram export files are analyzed on your device. Separate services process advertising, analytics and purchase data as explained below.

2. Instagram exports and local records

The app reads your selected Instagram ZIP locally to display follower and following lists, non-followers, pending requests, statistics, import history and comparisons. Results and preferences are stored in local app storage. Your ZIP and follower username lists are not sent to an analysis server. The app does not ask for your Instagram password.

The app attempts to delete its temporary ZIP copy after processing. Your original downloaded file remains where you saved it. Numerical counts and usage events can leave the device through analytics, as described next.

3. Analytics and campaign measurement

AppsFlyer measures installations, referrals and app use, and supports advertising fraud prevention. It processes app-install and device identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device/OS information, language, timestamps, launches and feature interactions.

Events include onboarding, imports, results/insights views, paywall and purchase actions, reminder choices, history and sharing actions. Events can include numerical follower, following, non-follower and lost-follower counts, whether a previous export exists and the number of exports in history. These events do not include your ZIP or imported follower username lists.

Campaign links can include media-source, campaign and creator identifiers. First-touch referral details are stored locally and can be sent to RevenueCat. A creator username identifies the campaign creator, rather than being read from your Instagram lists. AppsFlyer and RevenueCat identifiers are linked for subscription conversion and revenue measurement.

4. Advertising

The free app displays Google AdMob ads. Its SDK processes IP address/approximate location, advertising or other device identifiers, ads viewed and interacted with, app interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its service. Premium removes in-app ads; it does not disable purchase management or all analytics.

Ad personalization and access to advertising identifiers depend on platform permissions, applicable consent choices and advertising service settings. On iOS, system tracking permission controls access to the advertising identifier and permission for tracking. Denying tracking does not stop all service data collection. Manage tracking and advertising preferences in your iOS or Android privacy settings. Where a consent message is offered, its choices also apply to the processing described in that message.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Apple or Google handles payment through your store account. RevenueCat validates purchases, manages Premium access, restores purchases and measures subscription performance. It processes an app user identifier, transaction/receipt details, products, subscription status, purchase/renewal times, price/currency and technical device information. You do not enter payment card details into Unfollowers.

RevenueCat reports subscription events to AppsFlyer for campaign measurement. Associated data can include AppsFlyer and available advertising/device identifiers, campaign attributes, purchases, renewals and refunds. The RevenueCat project also has Apple Search Ads attribution enabled to measure acquisition from Apple advertising.

6. Website, support and sharing

Vercel hosts this website and processes network request information, including IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure it. Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights measure visits and performance, including page URL, referrer, browser/device type, country and performance metrics. Web Analytics does not use cookies for visitor measurement. Events can measure store-link clicks; store links can include campaign parameters.

Emailing support provides your email address and whatever message/attachments you send. Do not send passwords or your full Instagram export. When you choose to share a result card or open an external service, your selected content goes to that destination, which applies its own policy. Reminders are scheduled on your device and can be managed in app and notification settings.

7. Recipients and processing purposes

Google AdMob, AppsFlyer, RevenueCat, Apple/Google purchase services, Vercel and our email provider process information for the purposes described above. They and their subprocessors may process it outside your country. Their notices explain their processing and international transfer safeguards. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to address fraud, security incidents or legal claims.

We process data to provide requested app/purchase functions, answer support requests, improve reliability, understand usage, measure marketing and deliver ads. Where applicable law requires consent, that processing requires consent. Other processing relies on the applicable legal basis, such as providing a requested service, legal obligations or legitimate interests in reliability and fraud prevention. Rights and choices depend on your location and the processing involved.

8. Retention, deletion and your choices

Imported records remain locally until reset, removal of app data or uninstall, subject to platform backup behavior. Settings → Reset Data removes imported lists, related metadata, hidden-list entries and export history. It does not delete your original ZIP, cancel subscriptions, reset every preference or automatically erase service-provider records.

Purchase records are retained as needed for entitlement validation/restoration, refunds and legal/accounting obligations. Analytics and advertising records follow the relevant service’s retention settings and policies. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve requests and for related recordkeeping/legal obligations. Local reset does not delete these external records.

To request access, correction, deletion or information about your data, email tothecornerlabs@gmail.com with the subject “Unfollowers privacy request.” No app account is needed. Include your platform and request type. We may ask for proportionate information to locate records and verify a request. Do not send passwords or card details. Records needed for legal obligations, fraud prevention or unresolved claims may need to be retained; we will explain applicable limits.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent and complaint to your local data-protection authority. Manage/cancel subscriptions through your App Store or Google Play account. A privacy request or deleting local data does not cancel billing.

9. Security, children and updates

Local records rely on your device’s security protections. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and export files protected.

The service is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through the service so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

We may update this policy when the service or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Review this page for updates.
